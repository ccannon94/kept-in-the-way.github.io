I've been asked several times recently about God's omniscience. _"If God knows everything,"_ they ask, _"then He knows exactly what it would take for the people I pray for to come to faith. Why would he not just **do it**?"_

This is, after all, a fair question. I am not a part of a Calvinist tradition, so I believe that God desires all people to come to saving faith, limited only by His desire that they should be free to choose Him. Why then, would He not arrange the perfect encounter with just the right apologist so that those I pray for _would choose Him_?
