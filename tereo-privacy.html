Tereo Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Tereo is a private prayer app. Prayer intentions, prayer notes, prayer lists, prayer session history, and account information are treated as sensitive personal data and are used only to provide the app's private prayer-tracking and sync features.

Information We Collect

Tereo collects the information needed to create your account, protect your private workspace, and sync your data across your devices:

  • Account information: your email address, account identifier, and authentication session information.
  • Prayer content: prayer titles, optional descriptions, prayer lists, prayer notes, prayer status, and related timestamps.
  • Prayer session information: private session history, session status, included prayers, prayer-session timestamps, and related recap-ready facts.
  • Technical information needed for app functionality: network IP addresses, approximate city/country derived from network requests, account and authentication events, SDK/user-agent information, request results and errors, connection duration, transfer sizes, and operation counts retained by our authentication, database, and sync providers for security and reliability.

PowerSync also uses an identifier generated for each local database/client. It persists with that database and can change when the cache is recreated; it is not an advertising identifier or an immutable hardware ID. Technical information can be associated with your account. Tereo does not request device location permission or collect GPS location; approximate network location is distinct from precise device location.

How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • create and manage your private Tereo account;
  • save, display, update, delete, and sync your prayers, notes, lists, and prayer sessions;
  • keep your prayer data scoped to your authenticated account;
  • protect the security and reliability of the app; and
  • respond to privacy, support, or deletion requests you send to us.

Tereo does not sell your personal information. Tereo does not use prayer content, prayer notes, or prayer activity for advertising, third-party tracking, public sharing, AI processing, analytics, or marketing profiles.

Third-Party Service Providers

Tereo uses Supabase for authentication and cloud database services, and PowerSync for private sync between your device and the cloud database. These providers process Tereo data only as needed to provide account, storage, sync, security, and reliability features for the app.

Private Data And Device Storage

Tereo stores private app data in an encrypted, account-scoped local database with a device-local encryption key in Keychain. Offline changes can remain on the device until they sync. Signing out checks for pending changes; follow the app's prompt to sync them or explicitly discard them before removing that account's cache from the active device. A sign-out or deletion on one device does not establish immediate erasure of a disconnected device's local files.

Data Retention And Deletion

We keep your Tereo account data for as long as your account is active or as long as needed to provide the app's private sync features, unless you delete records sooner. You can initiate account deletion in Settings or email contact@keptintheway.org. Account deletion is handled through a manual support process after verification; submitting a request does not itself delete the account. After verifying the request, we will delete the active account data we control unless we need to retain limited information for legal, security, or abuse prevention reasons.

Our current Supabase Free service exposes API/database logs for one day and Auth audit logs for one hour. Our current PowerSync Free instance-log window is 24 hours. These are provider log-access windows, not a promise that every provider copy is erased at that time. Authentication/session records, sync operation history, and provider operational recovery/security copies have separate lifecycles. The current Supabase plan has no scheduled backups, point-in-time recovery, or log drains configured. PowerSync compacts operation history automatically; deletion of active data does not mean immediate removal from every history or provider-held recovery copy. We do not promise an unverified expiry period for those copies. Contact us for the current status of your deletion request and any applicable retention limit.

Children

Tereo is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Tereo, contact us and we will review the request.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy when Tereo's privacy practices change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or account/data deletion requests, email contact@keptintheway.org.
