Welcome to the "Kept in the Way" Blog. This blog is a spot for me to process, package, and share the results of my own personal study. Honestly, this is primarily for my own use, but if others find what I write here fruitful for their discipleship, I will surely praise God.

I don't think I can commit to any regular schedule of posting, as I am the father of two young girls and am fully committed to making time with them my first priority in these precious and formative years. But I pray that by God's grace this blog will be both a useful tool for me to digest and absorb what God has been teaching me through my own study, and that other's may benefit as well.
