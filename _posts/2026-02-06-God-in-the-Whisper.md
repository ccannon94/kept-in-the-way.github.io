# God in the Whisper

Imagine you know that you are going to encounter the almighty God of the universe, creator of heaven and earth.

You don't know what to expect when the presence of God is before you on the mountaintop, so you're ready for anything.

Suddenly, there is a great and strong wind ripping over the mountain top, your cloak snaps at your side and your feet briefly shuffle as you regain balance in the face of the mighty force of the wind. You lean into it, embracing the power of the wind that surely must herald the presence of God Almighty.

_But God Almighty was not in the wind._

As the wind around you calms, the very ground beneath your feet begins to quake. You feel the mass of the mountain that you are standing on suddenly feel like a bamboo raft tossed about on the waves. You are no longer confident that your feet can keep you on the mountaintop as it is tossed back and forth...

_But God Almighty was not in the earthquake._

Finally, you feel warmth spread across your face as the earth beneath your feet settles, and a pillar of fire descends on the mountain before you, hot and bright and seemingly full of divine power. The heat grows from gentle warmth to a slight discomfort and continues you climb. You begin to worry about every sinful thought that has ever crossed your mind, will the obvious and devouring power of God in the fire consume your sinful soul?

_But God Almight was not in the fire._

Then... a whisper.
